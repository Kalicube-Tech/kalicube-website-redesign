Why a strong brand in Google and AI speaks for you

One of our clients put it perfectly. He wants to sit in first class, talk to the person next to him and be completely humble, because he knows that when they get off the plane, they will look him (or his company) up. And Google and AI will do the boasting for him.

That is what taking control gives you. Once Google and AI have your story, your ideal clients and their intent straight, they understand you, trust you and recommend you with more conviction than you could ever decently use about yourself. Increasingly, AI agents then act on that recommendation for the people you want to reach.
