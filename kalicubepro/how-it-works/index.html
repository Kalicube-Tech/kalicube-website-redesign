THE QUESTION SETS

The questions we collect, one tab each

Every week we collect the answers Google and AI give to these sets. Kalicube mints each set for your brand. Reputation isn’t a tab of its own: it lives in Due Diligence, or becomes a Funnel Pathway when reputation is the intent.

Standard

The brand-name spine: who this is. The same shape for every brand.

Funnel Pathway

One audience and one intent, from the first question to the decision. Your name only appears in the decision-stage questions.

Due Diligence

The checks AI runs before it trusts you: are you real, licensed, reviewed, is there controversy. Your name is in every question.

Comparison

You against one named peer: how you’re different and how you’re similar. Two questions per peer, never the whole group.

AI Brand Perception

A diagnostic: what the machine currently believes about a theme, and what’s missing. Not a client FAQ.

Disambiguation

Making sure AI doesn’t confuse you with another entity with a similar name. Only when there’s a collision.

EVERY FEATURE · 1 · COLLECT · YOUR AUDIENCE AND YOUR MARKET

How AI perceives the group, not how to copy one company

We group the data around two claims: your Audience, who this is for, and your Active Market, the market you play in. Where the two cross is how you act correctly, address the right audience and position yourself according to the AI’s understanding of both.

You don’t chase one competitor. How AI perceives the competition as a group is how you act, and how it perceives the audience as a group is who you talk to, and where.
