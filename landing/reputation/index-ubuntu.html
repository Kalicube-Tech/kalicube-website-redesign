Pushing it down the page no longer works

Why traditional online reputation management fails in AI search

Traditional reputation management drowns the problem in content, hoping the bad result slips to page two. That worked when people scrolled through ten blue links. AI doesn’t scroll. It reads everything and writes one answer, and if the only detailed account of you is the bad one, that’s the one it repeats. Machines don’t count content. They look for the same answer in enough places, for long enough, to believe it.
